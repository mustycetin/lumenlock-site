Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store

Lights Out: Lumenlock is a calm, tactile light-toggling mosaic puzzle crafted with a stained-glass and lead-frame look. Tap a tile to flip it along with its edge-sharing neighbors across square, triangle, and hexagon boards.

Three Modes · 600 Solvable Levels

  • Classic (200 levels): Pure light puzzles across square, triangular, and hexagonal mosaics.
  • Seal (200 levels): Introduces a new mechanic every 20 levels—sealed tiles, portal pairs, and corner-pattern tiles.
  • Stained Glass (200 levels): Blend Red, Green, and Blue light brushes to craft the target stained-glass pattern and watch it glow in the dark.

Every puzzle is solvable, with 3-star scoring based on the shortest solution, guided interactive tutorials, Light Glass and Night Glass themes, original music, sound effects, haptics, and 8 languages. The first 20 levels of each mode are free; a single one-time Premium purchase unlocks all 600 levels. No ads, no subscriptions, no tracking, and fully playable offline.
